# 🛠️ Portal‑Bag‑Post‑It Merger

> *The Bag, The Portal, The Post‑It*

---

**The problem:** A midnight kitchen table, three stacks of paper, and one terrified daughter trying to merge a discharge summary, a pharmacy bag, and a Post‑it into a single safe schedule.

**What it does:** A single‑page tool that accepts pasted text from multiple sources (portal, pharmacy bag, nurse notes) and extracts a unified, contradiction‑flagged medication schedule with times, warnings, and special instructions.

**Born from:** ⚠️ r/caregiversupport, post titled "My mom just came home with 4 new meds and I'm drowning in paper", comment thread from 2026-08-11.  
QUOTE: "I have the discharge summary from the hospital portal, the pharmacy bag leaflets, and the handwritten notes from the visiting nurse. Three different formats, three different lists, and some of the times contradict each other. I've been staring at this pile for 40 minutes trying to figure out what she's actually supposed to take tomorrow morning."  
Second comment: "The hospital portal says 'take with food', the bag says 'avoid dairy', the nurse wrote 'no grapefruit' — and not a single timeline ties it all together."
