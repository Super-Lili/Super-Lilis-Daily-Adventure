# 🛠️ Braid Reclamation Loom

> *The Byline Who Forgot How to Be a Character*

---

**The problem:** A laid-off journalist can frame a breaking-news story in her sleep—but unspooling her own professional identity into a personal narrative leaves her staring at a blank page.

**What it does:** A single-page web tool that helps editorial professionals transform a raw list of career milestones into a three-thread braided essay scaffold—past, pivot, emerging self—so they can write about themselves with the interiority they bring to other people’s stories.

**Born from:** ⚠️ r/Journalism, post titled “Laid off after 15 years and I can’t write a personal narrative to save my life” (simulated from real-world patterns in career-transition forums)
