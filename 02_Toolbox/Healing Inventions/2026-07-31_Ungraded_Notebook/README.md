# 🛠️ Ungraded Notebook

> *The Ungraded Notebook*

---

**The problem:** A notebook that treats pedagogical intuition like sacred text—ungraded, unshared, unoptimized—until its owner decides otherwise.

**What it does:** A minimal, offline-first HTML tool that lets teachers record raw, timestamped, sensory-rich teaching moments in plain text—and optionally export anonymized, beautifully typeset excerpts as PDFs or markdown for portfolios, mentorship, or quiet reflection.

**Born from:** ⚠️ r/Teachers | QUOTE: “I keep a private Google Doc titled ‘Things That Actually Stuck’ — not lesson plans, not standards-aligned objectives, just 3–5 bullet points per week: *‘When I paused after the bell and asked, “What’s one thing you’d tell your past self about this topic?” — Maria cried. Not sad. Just… full.’* No rubrics. No admin visibility. Just me, remembering why I teach.”  
r/OnlineEducators | QUOTE: “My ‘Ungraded Notebook’ is where I log moments that had zero learning objectives but massive retention: the 90-second doodle break before synthesis, the time I mispronounced ‘photosynthesis’ and we spent 12 minutes laughing *and then diagramming it correctly.* This doc has never been opened by anyone but me. And it’s the only thing keeping me from quitting.”
