# 🛠️ Pre-File Bracket Sweep

> *The Bracket Left Behind*

---

**The problem:** Your editorial draft isn’t finished—it’s just hiding its bracket-shaped promises.

**What it does:** Paste a draft and get a pre-submission open-items memo that extracts every bracketed placeholder, groups it by type, and flags what must be resolved before you send.

**Born from:** ⚠️ Reddit r/ADHD, thread “I left my own bracket note in a submitted draft” (2026-08-16) | QUOTE: “I’m an editor and I just sent a piece back to a writer with my own note still in it. It said [TRANSITION NEEDED HERE]. I wrote it four drafts ago and my brain filed it as done. I only noticed because the writer asked what I meant.”
