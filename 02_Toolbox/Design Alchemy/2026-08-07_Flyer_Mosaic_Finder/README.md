# 🛠️ Flyer Mosaic Finder

> *Flyer Mosaic Finder*

---

**The problem:** What if you could browse your mess of homemade flyers and posters without opening a single file?

**What it does:** A single‑page visual browser that scans your messy folder of image exports and shows every flyer, poster, and card you've ever made—so you can find the right one in seconds.

**Born from:** ⚠️ Reddit (r/declutter) | QUOTE: "I spent 30 minutes this morning scrolling through my Downloads folder looking for the flyer I made for last year's charity bake sale. I know I named it something like 'final_final_bakesale_flyer_v3.png' but it's lost among 400 other 'final' files. I'm not a designer, just a PTA mom who made some Canva stuff, and now my folder is chaos. Any suggestions for finding old designs easily without uploading to Canva?"
