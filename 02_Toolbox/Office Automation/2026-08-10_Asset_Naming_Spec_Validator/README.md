# 🛠️ Asset Naming Spec Validator

> *The Name That Should Not Be*

---

**The problem:** A number from a design-handoff study shows that naming chaos costs more than we admit — and a checker that compares your spec to your actual exported filenames could pull that cost out of the dark.

**What it does:** A single‑page tool that lets a designer paste an expected naming convention pattern (e.g., “Button/Primary/Hover@2x”) and drag in a zip of exported assets, then instantly lists every mismatch and missing variant.

**Born from:** ⚠️ WORKTECH Academy, “Design-to-Dev Handoff Friction Audit Q2 2026,” published 2026-08-05.  
QUOTE: “Across 1,200 design-to-development handoffs studied in Q2 2026, 34% of exported assets had naming discrepancies that caused developers to pause and manually re-map filenames, costing an average of 22 minutes per batch. These interruptions cumulatively added 12 hours of lost productive time per project, with a median cost impact of $4,100.”
