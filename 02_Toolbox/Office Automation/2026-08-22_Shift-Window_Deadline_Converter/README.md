# 🛠️ Shift-Window Deadline Converter

> *The Deadline That Falls While You Sleep*

---

**The problem:** Because “end of day” should not mean “while you are unconscious.”

**What it does:** A shift-aware deadline converter that turns 9-to-5 editorial calendars into personal wake/sleep windows and generates ready-to-send early-submission or extension notes.

**Born from:** ⚠️ Reddit r/Newsletters, post “Night-shift newsletter writers: how do you handle submission windows?” | QUOTE: “I write my newsletter between 2 and 5 a.m. because that’s when I’m awake, but every submission call, every sponsorship pitch, every editor’s office hours are all 9 to 5. I’ve missed three deadlines this month because ‘end of day’ means while I’m unconscious.”
