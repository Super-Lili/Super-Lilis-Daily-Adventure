# 🛠️ Pre-Meeting Intent Memo

> *The “Why Did We Even Meet?” Memo Generator*

---

**The problem:** A tool that helps teams co-author a 90-second pre-meeting memo — so the meeting either earns its place or gracefully dissolves.

**What it does:** A minimal, single-page HTML tool that guides two or more collaborators to jointly draft a 3-field pre-meeting memo (Purpose, Required Decisions, Owner-by-Outcome) — then exports it as a timestamped, shareable plain-text snippet for pasting into calendar invites or Slack.

**Born from:** ⚠️ Microsoft Work Trend Index 2026 Midyear Report (published 2026-07-18) | QUOTE: "Among knowledge workers who attended ≥5 recurring meetings per week, 68% reported at least one ‘ritual meeting’—a session with no documented agenda, no defined owner, and no measurable outcome. When asked what would improve these meetings, the top response was not better tools—but a shared, lightweight artifact to justify or cancel them *before* the calendar invite fires."
