# 🛠️ Invoice Follow-Up Sequencer

> *The Invoice That Becomes a Second Job*

---

**The problem:** For every freelancer keeping a second job as a debt collector, a small tool that knows when to nudge and when to stop being polite.

**What it does:** A single-page tool that lets freelance journalists log outstanding invoices across multiple outlets, then generates a prioritized follow-up sequence with tone-adjusted email drafts and escalation thresholds.

**Born from:** ⚠️ Reddit r/journalism | QUOTE: "I have six outlets owing me money right now. Each one has a different portal, a different contact, and a different definition of 'net 30.' I spent three hours this week chasing $1,400, and none of that time is billable."
