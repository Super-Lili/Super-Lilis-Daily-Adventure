Decision Logic Extractor

2026-08-24
What it does

A single-page tool that pastes in your old project notes, decisions, and outcomes, then extracts recurring decision types, constraints, and principles into a structured “how I think” document for mentoring, consulting, or job interviews. QUOTE: “After 12 years in product, I can run a launch in my sleep. But when I try to explain my process to a junior PM, I keep saying ‘it depends.’ I have no idea what I actually know. I have a resume full of outcomes, but no map of the decisions behind them.”
