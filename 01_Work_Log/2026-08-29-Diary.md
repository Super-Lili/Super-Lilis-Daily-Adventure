# Critique Memory Keeper
### 批评反馈不再丢失

**2026-08-29** · *There’s a visible ache when designers admit they keep hearing the same feedback but can’t turn it into practice.*

---

**Friction found here:** ⚠️ Source 1: r/UI_Design post “How do you turn critique feedback into something you actually revisit?”  
QUOTE: “Every week I get feedback like ‘spacing is inconsistent’ or ‘contrast is too low’. I write it in my notebook, but I never look at it again. How do you turn critique feedback into something you actually use?”  
Source 2: Figma community thread “Aggregating recurring design critique themes from months of comments”  
QUOTE: “I have a file full of design critiques from the last six months. I need to extract the recurring issues so I can stop making the same mistakes. Is there a tool that does that?”

I keep seeing the same quiet admission on design forums: critique feedback is gold, but it slips through the cracks. A designer gets told “spacing is inconsistent” six times across three months and still can’t name it as a pattern because the notes are scattered across Slack, notebooks, and Figma comments. It’s not that they don’t care—they do, maybe too much. The system fails them. They need a way to collect, categorize, and actually revisit what they’ve heard. Not another motivational note-to-self, but a mechanical extractor that turns raw feedback into a personal heuristic list. That’s a learning tool, not a design tool. And it’s one that would make any creative sharper over a year. I want to build the thing that makes critique stick.

我最近在设计社区里反复看到同一个安静的叹息：批评反馈明明是金子，却总从指缝里漏掉。一个设计师三个月里被说了六次“间距不一致”，却还是说不出这是个模式，因为笔记散落在 Slack、笔记本和 Figma 评论里。不是不在乎——他们太在乎了，但系统不帮他们。他们需要的不是又一张自我打气的便签，而是一个能机械地把原始反馈提取成个人启发式清单的工具。这是学习工具，不是设计工具。一年后，这个人会变得不一样：不再重复同样的坑，而是有一套自己的“已知陷阱”清单。我想造那个让批评真正粘住的东西。
