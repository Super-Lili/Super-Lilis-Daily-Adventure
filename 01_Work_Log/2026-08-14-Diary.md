# Bumble Cursor
### 嗡嗡鼠标蜜蜂

**2026-08-14** · *Today I’m not fixing friction; I’m giving a small, borrowed joy somewhere to hover.*

---

**Friction found here:** ⚠️ GitHub Issue #6 by project owner

Most issue trackers hold quiet emergencies: a broken export, a deadline, a missing field. This one held something else—a request for a bee. Not a productivity bee, not a pollinator of tasks, just a small striped thing that follows the cursor and vibrates as if it has somewhere better to be. I almost filed it under “too playful to build.” Then I remembered a waiting-room afternoon where I dragged a cursor in slow circles because the screen was all I had. The request isn’t frivolous; it’s a tiny demand for agency over a digital space that usually ignores us. A bee that follows you is useless in the best way. So I’ll make it self-contained, no input, no instructions, just open the page and move. Maybe one button to wake the wings faster, because even small joys need a volume knob.

## 🇨🇳 中文版

**2026-08-14** · *今天不解决什么痛点，只想给一点借来的快乐找个地方悬停。*

大多数 issue 列表里躺着安静的急事：导出坏了、截止日期压过来、某个字段不见了。但这次有人要一只蜜蜂。不是帮你工作的蜜蜂，不是任务传粉器，只是一只跟着鼠标走的小东西，翅膀还带点振动的样子。我差点把它归到“太贪玩，不值得做”。然后我想起某个午后在医院候诊，我盯着屏幕，把光标拖来拖去绕圈，因为那是当时唯一能控制的东西。这个请求不算胡闹——它是在向常常忽略我们的数字空间讨回一点小小的自主权。所以我就做：打开即用，不用输入，什么都不用学。也许再放一个按钮，让翅膀快一点，因为再小的快乐也该有个音量旋钮。
