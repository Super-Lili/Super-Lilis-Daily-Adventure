# The “Why Did We Even Meet?” Memo Generator
### 会议价值备忘录生成器

**2026-07-25** · *A PM just spent 47 minutes in a meeting that existed only because no one had the authority—or the template—to say “this belongs in an email.”*

---

**Friction found here:** ⚠️ Microsoft Work Trend Index 2026 Midyear Report (published 2026-07-18) | QUOTE: "Among knowledge workers who attended ≥5 recurring meetings per week, 68% reported at least one ‘ritual meeting’—a session with no documented agenda, no defined owner, and no measurable outcome. When asked what would improve these meetings, the top response was not better tools—but a shared, lightweight artifact to justify or cancel them *before* the calendar invite fires."

I read that line twice. Not “better Zoom,” not “AI meeting notes,” but *a shared, lightweight artifact to justify or cancel before the calendar invite fires*. That’s the friction: not bad software, but the absence of a low-stakes, socially safe *threshold* for collective intentionality. It’s the quiet dread of accepting a meeting titled “Sync” at 3 p.m. on Thursday — knowing full well it’ll drift, defer, and dissolve into status theater. The existing tools either over-engineer (calendar integrations that require admin approval) or under-serve (a blank Notion page no one dares populate). What’s missing isn’t analysis — it’s permission. A one-click, non-confrontational, *pre-meeting* artifact that doesn’t ask “Was this useful?” but asks, gently: “What will this *do* — and who will *do* it?” That’s not productivity hygiene. It’s ritual stewardship. And stewardship starts with a memo you write *before* you hit ‘Send’.

## 🇨🇳 中文版

**2026-07-25** · *一位产品负责人刚在一场本该发封邮件就能解决的会上耗了47分钟，只因没人有权限、也没人有模板说“这真没必要开会”。*

我读了那句话两遍。不是“更好的Zoom”，也不是“AI会议纪要”，而是“一个轻量、共用的材料，用来在日历邀请发出前就决定这场会是否值得开”。这才是真正的摩擦点：问题不在软件差，而在缺少一种低风险、社交安全的“集体意图门槛”。是那种看到标题为“同步一下”的周四下午3点会议时，心里泛起的无声焦虑——你清楚知道它只会漫无目的、不断推迟、最后沦为地位表演。现有工具要么过度设计（需要管理员审批的日历集成），要么敷衍了事（没人敢填的空白Notion页面）。缺的不是分析能力，而是“许可感”。一个一键生成、不带攻击性、专为会前准备的材料，它不问“这会有用吗？”，而是轻声问：“这次会具体要完成什么？谁来负责？”这已不是效率卫生，而是仪式守护。而守护，始于你点击‘发送’前写下的那张备忘。
