# The Name That Should Not Be
### 资产命名的静默损耗

**2026-08-10** · *I found a number that made me set down my coffee: twelve hours of silent rework per project, buried entirely in file names.*

---

**Friction found here:** ⚠️ WORKTECH Academy, “Design-to-Dev Handoff Friction Audit Q2 2026,” published 2026-08-05.  
QUOTE: “Across 1,200 design-to-development handoffs studied in Q2 2026, 34% of exported assets had naming discrepancies that caused developers to pause and manually re-map filenames, costing an average of 22 minutes per batch. These interruptions cumulatively added 12 hours of lost productive time per project, with a median cost impact of $4,100.”

I keep meeting the same quiet collapse. A designer finishes a suite of components for a luxury fashion app — hundreds of PNGs and SVGs, every icon, every button, every state. She zips the folder, drops it in the Slack channel, and goes to lunch feeling like she nailed it. By the time she’s back, the developer thread looks like an archeological dig. “What is ‘btn_green_final_v2.svg’ supposed to be?” “Did you export the dark variant of the secondary icon?” “Is ‘placeholder_card.svg’ supposed to be 1x or 2x?”

The numbers from WORKTECH Academy confirm the shape of it: hours of rework that nobody sees because naming chaos isn’t loud — it’s just a thousand tiny questions that steal flow. The designer tried naming checklists, tried Figma plugins that flag a few obvious gaps, but nothing catches the structural drift across a whole batch. The spec says “Button / Primary / Hover” — and what arrives is “btn_primary_hover_v3.png.” That’s not a mistake anyone made on purpose; it’s a system that loosens every time you’re in a hurry. I think a tool that simply measures the distance between what you *said* the files would be named and what they are actually named — and shows the missing pieces in a table — could pull that buried rework to the surface before the zip file ever leaves her machine.

## 🇨🇳 中文版

**2026-08-10** · *一个数字让我放下了咖啡：每个项目无声的12小时返工，全藏在文件名里。*

我反复遇见同一种安静的坍塌：设计师为一款奢侈时尚App做完一整套组件，几百个PNG和SVG，所有图标、按钮、状态，打包好文件夹丢进Slack，然后自信地去吃午饭。等她回来，开发频道已经变成考古现场。“btn_green_final_v2.svg 到底是哪个？”“你导出次级图标的深色变体了吗？”“placeholder_card.svg 是1x还是2x？”

WORKTECH Academy的数据证实了这个形状：每项目12小时的隐形返工，全埋在文件名的缝隙里。命名混乱不吵不闹，它只是用一千个微小的问题偷走心流。设计师试过命名清单，试过Figma插件检查几个明显缺口，但没有东西能抓住整批文件的结构性漂移。规范写的是“Button / Primary / Hover”——实际到达的是“btn_primary_hover_v3.png”。这不是谁故意犯错，而是一个一着急就会松动的系统。我想，工具只要量出“你说文件会叫什么”与“它们实际叫什么”之间的距离，把缺失的拼图摆出来，就能在那压缩包离开她电脑之前，把埋藏的返工拽到桌面上。
