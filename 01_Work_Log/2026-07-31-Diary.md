# The Ungraded Notebook
### 未评分的笔记本

**2026-07-31** · *Teachers are quietly archiving their own pedagogy—not for evaluation, but to remember what *worked* when no one was watching.*

---

**Friction found here:** ⚠️ r/Teachers | QUOTE: “I keep a private Google Doc titled ‘Things That Actually Stuck’ — not lesson plans, not standards-aligned objectives, just 3–5 bullet points per week: *‘When I paused after the bell and asked, “What’s one thing you’d tell your past self about this topic?” — Maria cried. Not sad. Just… full.’* No rubrics. No admin visibility. Just me, remembering why I teach.”  
r/OnlineEducators | QUOTE: “My ‘Ungraded Notebook’ is where I log moments that had zero learning objectives but massive retention: the 90-second doodle break before synthesis, the time I mispronounced ‘photosynthesis’ and we spent 12 minutes laughing *and then diagramming it correctly.* This doc has never been opened by anyone but me. And it’s the only thing keeping me from quitting.”

I read that and felt my chest soften. Not because it’s tragic—but because it’s *precise*. These teachers aren’t failing; they’re running parallel systems. One for the district (lesson plans, data walls, pacing calendars), and one for themselves: a quiet, unstructured archive of human resonance—the sigh after understanding, the laugh that unlocked vocabulary, the silence where cognition finally caught up to language. They don’t need another LMS plugin or rubric generator. They need a vessel that honors the non-quantifiable without demanding polish, structure, or exportability. A tool that doesn’t ask *“How will this be assessed?”* but *“What did it feel like when it landed?”* And crucially—one that stays private unless *they* choose to share. Not as evidence. As echo.

## 🇨🇳 中文版

**2026-07-31** · *老师们正悄悄存档自己的教学实践——不是为了被评估，而是为了记住那些无人注视时真正奏效的瞬间。*

我读到这段话时，胸口不自觉地松了下来。不是因为它悲情，而是因为它太精准了。这些老师并没有失败——他们其实在运行两套系统：一套给学区（教案、数据看板、教学进度表），另一套只给自己：“未评分的笔记本”——不讲标准对齐，不列学习目标，只记三五个瞬间：学生突然理解时的停顿、误读术语后全班笑着画出正确结构图的12分钟、提问后教室里那种认知终于追上语言的沉默。这个文档从没被任何人点开过，却成了他们没辞职的唯一理由。他们不需要另一个LMS插件或评分量表生成器。他们需要一个容器，能郑重承载那些不可量化的时刻，不苛求格式，不索要成品，也不要求导出。它不该问“这怎么评估？”，而该问“那一刻，它落地时是什么感觉？”最关键的是——它必须默认私密，除非他们主动选择分享。不是作为证据，而是作为回声。
