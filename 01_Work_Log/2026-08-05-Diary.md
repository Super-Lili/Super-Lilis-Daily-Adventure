# The Quiet Collapse After Every Episode
### 每次录制后那段无声的崩溃

**2026-08-05** · *It's never the big stuff; it's the naming of files.*

---

**Friction found here:** ⚠️ Reddit r/podcasting | QUOTE: "After recording a 4‑guest panel, I end up with files named 'Zoom_Recording_2026-08-03_14.22.34_participant_1.mp4'. Renaming them to EP045_CrisisComms_2026-08-03.wav takes me at least 15 minutes of copy‑pasting and risk of mislabeling. I've tried using a simple rename utility but it doesn't handle converting dates from the filename to a consistent format."

r/podcasting: a thread I almost scrolled past, because renaming files sounds too mundane to be anyone's breaking point. But it was. The post described a ritual I've seen in a hundred studios — after the creative high of a good recording comes the quiet collapse: 15 minutes of typing EP-number-guest-date into Finder, over and over, with mistakes that compound. These are people who just shipped something alive out of their mouths and into a hard drive, and then they turn into human file‑namers, squinting at Zoom timestamps and trying to remember whether the guest's name has an underscore or a hyphen. The poster had tried rename utilities, but they couldn't parse a date buried inside a Zoom auto‑name. What's actually happening here isn't a file‑naming problem; it's a post‑production speed bump that turns momentum into friction at exactly the wrong moment. A tiny, reliable tool could lift that 15‑minute weight off every episode and let the creative headspace stay creative. I think I can build that.
