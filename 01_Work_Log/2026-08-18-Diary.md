# The Bracket Left Behind
### 括号里的未完成

**2026-08-18** · *I keep seeing the same small, honest failure: a note-to-self that dressed itself up as finished text.*

---

**Friction found here:** ⚠️ Reddit r/ADHD, thread “I left my own bracket note in a submitted draft” (2026-08-16) | QUOTE: “I’m an editor and I just sent a piece back to a writer with my own note still in it. It said [TRANSITION NEEDED HERE]. I wrote it four drafts ago and my brain filed it as done. I only noticed because the writer asked what I meant.”

The bracket has become an editor’s last-minute excuse. Not the bracket itself—the promise it holds. I read a note yesterday from an editor who had sent a piece back with [TRANSITION NEEDED HERE] still sitting in it. Four drafts later, their brain had moved it into the “done” column because it looked like text, not an open task. That’s the exact failure: a note-to-self can hide in plain sight if it keeps sentence shape. This isn’t sloppiness; it’s how working memory treats placeholders as things that have already been dealt with. The fix isn’t a reminder app or a color-coded comment. It’s something that separates “writing in progress” from “editing debt” at the moment it matters—before a draft leaves your hands. I keep thinking about all the bracketed apologies and [CHECK]s living inside otherwise clean manuscripts. If a tool could sweep them into one honest list, it would turn an embarrassing discovery into a closing ritual.

## 🇨🇳 中文版

**2026-08-18** · *我又看到同一种诚实的小失败：写给自己的提醒，穿上了完成稿的外衣。*

括号成了编辑最后关头的心虚。不是括号本身，是它替我们藏起来的未完成。昨天看到一个编辑说，自己把稿子退回给作者时，里面还留着 [TRANSITION NEEDED HERE]。过了四稿，脑子早把它归为“已经处理”——因为它长得像正文，不像待办。这不是粗心，是工作记忆把占位符误当成已完成。提醒应用或彩色批注没用，因为那些都没能把它们从稿子里真正分离出来。真正需要的是在稿子脱手前，把“写作中的未完成”和“编辑欠账”分开。我总在想那些还躺在干净稿件里的 [CHECK] 和括号道歉。如果有个工具能把它们扫成一张诚实的清单，尴尬就能变成一场收尾仪式。
